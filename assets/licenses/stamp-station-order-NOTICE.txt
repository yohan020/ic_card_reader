Railway-section geometry and station segments were contracted into connection
graphs. Non-station dead ends were pruned. This is not a numerical station-code
sort: N02 codes are geographic identifiers, not travel-order numbers.

Supplementary station-list facts: Japanese Wikipedia contributors, retrieved
2026-11-16. Text/database-derived ordering adaptations are attributed under
CC BY-SA 4.0; no images, logos, maps or article prose are included.
https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:著作権
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Consulted article titles, permanent page identifiers, URLs and response hashes
are recorded in docs/research/station-stamps-2026-11-16-order/order.json.
Each article URL provides its contributors and revision history. That manifest,
the derived station-order dataset and its generated order map are offered under
CC BY-SA 4.0 to the extent copyright applies. This does not relicense the
separately permissioned IC-code database or the application as a whole.

Changes: membership-scoped extraction, independently reviewed branches and
switchbacks, removal of incidental/historical station tables, joins at shared
terminals, and mapping to the app's existing station identities. Existing
official passenger boundary research is also reused. Order is independent of
the user's station-name language. No raw card history was used.

The collection catalog remains a snapshot, not a real-time railway timetable.
Separate sections and station order do not guarantee a particular train stops
at every station, a direct train, current IC availability, or current operation.
