Wikidata Korean station labels

This app includes a generated mapping of Japanese station names to Korean
labels supplied by Wikidata structured data. The generated mapping is used
only when a Japanese label has one unambiguous Korean label; otherwise the
original Japanese station name remains displayed.

Wikidata structured data is available under the Creative Commons CC0 Public
Domain Dedication. Source: https://www.wikidata.org/

Generated by tool/generate_wikidata_station_korean_labels.mjs.
