Processed from MLIT National Land Numerical Information — Railway data.
「国土数値情報（鉄道データ）」（国土交通省）を加工して作成
https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/
N02-24 is included through Japan Station Master; N02-25 was used to verify
the identity of selected app stations. This is not an official MLIT product.
Wikidata structured English labels in the source dataset are CC0.
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing
Existing Fukuoka City Subway station-name data (CC BY, adapted and matched):
https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/40130-009
For some historical and otherwise missing names, Wikipedia contributors'
article titles were matched by the same Wikidata item, then trimmed to the
station name. Individual historical names were also checked against the
line history. Article links are recorded in the coverage report; no article
body is included. Wikipedia articles: CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: the app selected and matched names to its own station identities,
omitted machine romanizations, normalized some display punctuation and
added individually reviewed public station names. No operator web page,
map, image or route document is reproduced. Existing third-party data terms,
including the Yoiko station-code permission, are unchanged.

ODPT, its data-providing operators and gachi-tokusuru do not guarantee the
accuracy or completeness of these data. For upstream dataset inquiries,
contact gachi-tokusuru at https://api.gachi-tokusuru.com rather than the
railway operators.
