Coin locker external links / 2026-11-03

The app includes independently curated place names, area descriptions and public
article URLs, not a reproduction of locker articles, photographs or maps.
No vacancy, reservation, price or complete facility inventory is included.
The services are independent third parties, not affiliated official partners.

Links open in an external browser only after the user's selection. Destination
services apply their own privacy policies. No card IDm, balance, transaction
history, location, user search term or other user data is appended by this app.
An old, partial or related-area article must not be treated as current complete
station coverage. Research and URL provenance remain in docs/research/
coin-locker-links-2026-11-03/; current generator:
tool/expand_coin_locker_catalog.mjs (historical seed builder is not the updater).
