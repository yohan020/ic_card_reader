# Kyoto Subway & Bus 1-Day Pass data notice

The in-app Kyoto Municipal Subway station, inter-station-distance, and fare-band data is a processed work based on the Kyoto City Open Data Portal dataset “京都市営地下鉄 駅間距離・運賃”.

- Provider: Kyoto City
- License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.ja)
- Source: <https://data.city.kyoto.lg.jp/dataset/00024/>
- Fare-band reference: <https://www2.city.kyoto.lg.jp/kotsu/webguide/ja/fare/fare_tika.html>
- Pass reference: <https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/kotsu/page/0000028378.html>

This app shows adult fares for planning only. Bus amounts are user-entered estimates (standard Kyoto City Bus ¥230 and sightseeing express bus ¥500) and do not identify the actual route, operator, adjusted-fare area, excluded route, or surcharge.
